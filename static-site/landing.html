Questions worth asking before you hand over a file.

Is FluxSend open source?

Yes. FluxSend is available under the MIT license, and the backend source is public on GitHub.

Do recipients need a FluxSend account?

Only for targeted sharing between registered users. Quick share links can be opened by people outside your instance.

Where are the files stored?

FluxSend supports self hosted MinIO, AWS S3, and Google Cloud Storage. Your instance chooses the provider.

Can I run FluxSend on my own server?

Yes. The project includes Docker Compose configurations, including a MinIO setup that avoids a managed cloud storage dependency.

Can I pay for a hosted plan?

Not yet. The main FluxSend SaaS instance shows the available plan tiers, but online billing is not live. Payment support and paid plan pricing are planned; self hosted instances assign plans through their administrator.

Are file downloads exposed publicly?

Files remain private until shared. Downloads resolve through short lived signed URLs rather than direct public bucket access.
